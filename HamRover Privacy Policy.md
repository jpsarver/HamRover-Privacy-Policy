# HamRover Privacy Policy

Effective date: Nov 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the HamRover iOS app ("HamRover," "the app") handles information. HamRover is developed and published by Paul Sarver ("we," "us").

**The short version:** HamRover does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

## Information the app stores

HamRover is a logging tool for amateur radio rover operating. To do that job, the app stores the following on your device:

- Your station information, such as your call sign and operating settings
- Contact (QSO) log entries, such as the other station's call sign, grid square, band, date and time
- Your planned rover stops and their Maidenhead grid squares, which you enter yourself

Call signs and grid squares are identifiers used openly on the air and in public licensing records. Even so, we treat everything in your log as your information, and we do not collect any of it.

## How information is stored

All app data is stored locally on your device. HamRover has no user accounts, no servers, and no sign-in. We cannot see, access, or recover your log data.

HamRover does not access your device's location or GPS. Grid squares come only from what you enter. The app also makes no network requests, including call sign lookups to online databases.

Your data is covered by your device's own protections (passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and device encryption) and by your device backups, if you use iCloud Backup or computer backups. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy and your own settings, not by us.

## Information we do not collect

HamRover does not:

- Send your data to us or to any server
- Use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools
- Include third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data
- Track you across other apps or websites
- Sell or share your information with anyone

HamRover has no crash reporting of its own. If you choose to share crash reports or diagnostics with app developers through your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash and performance data. This is controlled by you in iOS Settings and is handled under Apple's privacy policy.

## Exporting your data

HamRover lets you export your log (for example, to submit to a contest sponsor or to import into other logging software). Export happens only when you choose to do it, and you choose where the file goes, such as Files, Mail, AirDrop, or another app.

Once a file leaves HamRover, it is outside our control. Where it goes and how it is protected is up to you and to the services you send it to, under their own privacy policies. Note that contest logs submitted to a sponsor may be published as part of the contest results.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual log entries within the app. Deleting HamRover from your device removes all of its data from that device. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

## Children's privacy

HamRover does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If HamRover's handling of information changes (for example, if a future version adds cloud sync or online call sign lookups), we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released.
